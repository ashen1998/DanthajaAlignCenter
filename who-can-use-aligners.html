Suitability

Clear Aligner Treatment Designed Around Your Smile.

Clear aligners are transparent, removable trays that gradually move teeth into improved alignment. They are often chosen by patients who want a more discreet alternative to traditional braces. At Danthaja Aligner Center, every case begins with a professional consultation to check suitability, understand your goals, and design a safe treatment plan.

Common concerns

Concerns aligners may help with

Crowded teeth

Where teeth overlap or sit unevenly, planned movement may help create space.

Gaps between teeth

Spaces that may be closed gradually, where clinically appropriate.

Mild to moderate misalignment

Teeth that are slightly rotated, tipped or out of line.

Relapse after braces

Teeth that have shifted after previous braces or orthodontic treatment.

Smile appearance concerns

Concerns that require guided tooth movement rather than cosmetic work alone.
