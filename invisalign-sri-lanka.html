Premium Invisalign FAQs

Questions about Invisalign

Dr Jeremy Perera is based in Melbourne, Australia, and works with Danthaja Aligner Center as Remote Premium Invisalign Lead. He visits the centre once every two months, and collaborates with the local team between visits.

Your reviews and follow-up are supported locally by the Danthaja team, with coordination by Dr Surain Gomez, so you have attentive support throughout treatment.

Not every case is suitable for clear aligners. Some bite problems or complex movements may need alternative orthodontic care. A suitability assessment helps identify the best option.

Pricing is confirmed after your clinical assessment, once your plan and timeline are clear. It reflects specialist planning and the standards of a globally recognised aligner system.
