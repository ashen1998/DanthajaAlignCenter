Danthaja In-House Aligners vs Premium Invisalign: Which Pathway May Suit You?

Danthaja Aligner Center·6 min read·Reviewed by: [Clinician name — placeholder]

If you are exploring clear aligners in Sri Lanka, you have probably noticed two very different conversations: one about accessibility and convenience, and another about global brands and specialist planning. At Danthaja Aligner Center, we keep these as two distinct pathways — and help you choose based on suitability.

Pathway 1: Danthaja In-House Aligners

Danthaja In-House Aligners are a locally managed, technology-driven pathway designed for patients with mild orthodontic concerns. Treatment is coordinated through our in-house digital workflow, with clinical oversight by Dr Surain Gomez.

This pathway may suit you if you have mild spacing, mild crowding, or relapse after previous orthodontic treatment, and you would like a practical, competitively positioned, and locally supported solution. It is often considered by university students, young professionals, and couples planning a wedding.

Pathway 2: Premium Invisalign

Premium Invisalign is led by Dr Jeremy Perera for patients who want the reassurance of a globally recognised aligner system and a high-trust clinical experience. The focus is on specialist treatment planning, international standards, and discreet adult orthodontics.

It is often chosen by executives, public-facing professionals, expatriates, and overseas Sri Lankans visiting home — though, again, suitability is confirmed through assessment.

The right pathway is the one that suits your teeth, bite, goals, and lifestyle — not simply the price, the brand, or how severe you think your case is.

How to think about the choice

  • Case complexity: some movements may be better suited to one pathway, and some cases may need alternative orthodontic care.
  • Your priorities: local convenience and accessibility, or a globally recognised system with specialist planning.
  • Your timeline: weddings, travel, or work commitments may shape the plan.
  • Ongoing care: both pathways include progress reviews and retainer guidance.

Start with an assessment

A consultation is the clearest way to understand your options. We examine your teeth, bite, and oral health, discuss your goals, and explain which pathway may suit you — with an estimated timeline and pricing confirmed after assessment.
