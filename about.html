Our journey

The dream behind the expansion

Danthaja’s expansion is not only a business milestone. It is a dream carried forward by people who trusted each other, grew with each other, and believed that Sri Lanka deserves a new standard of smile, skin, and facial confidence care.

At its foundation is friendship. Mr Chatura Kalansooriya and Mr Sanjaya Gamage, Royal College schoolmates and founding directors of Danthaja Private Limited, helped build the company on shared trust and responsibility. That same foundation now supports Vathraja Private Limited, whose shares are held by Danthaja Private Limited.

Dr Surain Gomez’s journey makes this story deeply personal. He was once an intern under Dr Lakmal Kulasekara at Badulla Hospital; today, he is a respected private practitioner and aligner provider. His growth reflects what Danthaja believes most: when talent is mentored with trust, it can become something bigger than itself.

The same spirit continues through Dr Jeremy Perera, a close friend and batchmate of Dr Lakmal, whose involvement in Danthaja’s Premium Invisalign pathway brings friendship, loyalty, and a shared pursuit of brilliance in Sri Lankan dentistry.

For Danthaja, this is the true meaning of expansion: to create space for people, partnerships, and ideas to grow into a new future for dentofacial aesthetics in Sri Lanka.

“A dream built on trust — between friends, families, teachers, mentors, and the next generation of talent. When the right people come together with sincerity, they can create something beautiful for the future of care in Sri Lanka.”
