Pathway 1 · Danthaja In-House Aligners

Accessible Clear Aligner Treatment, Locally Managed by Danthaja.

This pathway is designed for patients with mild orthodontic concerns who want a practical, competitively priced, and locally supported clear aligner solution. Danthaja In-House Aligners highlight convenience, digital scanning, quick follow-up, local treatment support, and clinical oversight by Dr Surain Gomez.

Best suited for

Mild concerns, thoughtfully managed

Based on assessment, Danthaja In-House Aligners may be suitable for:

  • Mild spacing between teeth
  • Mild crowding
  • Relapse after previous orthodontic treatment
  • Patients seeking a discreet smile improvement

Suitability is confirmed only after a clinical assessment. If your case needs more complex movement, we will explain other options — including Premium Invisalign or alternative orthodontic care.

Expectations & limitations

Honest expectations, from the start

  • Designed for mild spacing, mild crowding and relapse — not complex bite problems.
  • Results depend on wearing aligners as advised and attending reviews.
  • Treatment time depends on case complexity, compliance, and the number of aligners required.
  • If your case needs more, we will explain Premium Invisalign or alternative orthodontic care.
